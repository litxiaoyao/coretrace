\paragraph{Latent multi-hop composition.}
Correct constituent facts need not yield a correct composed answer \citep{press2023compositionality}. \citet{biran2024hopping} identify delayed bridge resolution, but their back-patching rescue rate selects a successful layer pair retrospectively for each example. \citet{yu2025back} diagnose competing relation-conditioned logits and add trainable cross-layer attention to feed deeper representations into earlier computation. \citet{liu2026layerorder} replace strict hop ordering with probabilistic recall and selective extraction, but do not evaluate a mechanism-derived repair. These parametric-retrieval studies do not distinguish reaching a supplied evidence paragraph from reading the relation-relevant content within it.

\paragraph{Contextual evidence use.}
\citet{guo2025twohop} reconstruct a sequential-query circuit in a three-layer synthetic Transformer; that circuit is not causally established in the pretrained LLMs used for their behavioral experiments. \citet{zhang2026failure} identify a position-dependent recognition bottleneck through instructions naming gold documents. Their intervention supplies oracle evidence locations and reduces the search space, so it does not independently isolate recognition from synthesis. Its attention visualizations do not manipulate the underlying neural operations. Our shared-context interventions factor attention into paragraph mass and a normalized distribution within that paragraph while retaining receiver value/output computations. Reciprocal object exchanges keep global answer counts fixed and test whether predictions follow the selected paragraph's content.

\paragraph{Circuit-guided repair.}
\citet{sun2026bridging} trace multilingual failures to misaligned attention pathways over shared semantic neurons and fine-tune critical heads. \citet{kim2026bridge} identify multilingual bridge-routing heads and recover parametric failures by amplifying their outputs. \citet{sun2025mappings} localize demonstration-to-label matching and improve in-context classification through targeted head tuning. These interventions do not separate evidence-paragraph access from conditional reading within that paragraph. We test this separation in contextual composition, where an address can remain linearly decodable despite answer failure. Decodability motivates the availability--use distinction; the factorized intervention tests the interaction between access and reading. CoReTrace operationalizes the address signal using an input-derived title graph and a training-fitted readout, then combines selected original paragraphs with scoped head adaptation. Its inference inputs require neither support annotations nor answer labels or donor activations. Model-specific value/output restoration constrains the parameter scope retained after adaptation.

## Selected scientific narrative

**Correct constituent answers can coexist with a decodable bridge address and a failed composed answer. The central diagnostic finding is that paragraph access changes the utility of the local attention pattern.** CoReTrace turns the recoverable address into evidence selection and combines it with adaptation of the implicated heads. Separate content exchanges verify that the resulting answers respond to local fact bindings.

This is the most distinctive story supported by the existing experiments. A story centered on sparse tuning repeats a broader established intervention pattern; a story centered on a complete reasoning circuit exceeds the available localization evidence. The functional access–pattern interaction is directly manipulable and quantified in the existing four-arm experiment.

## Evidence chain and boundaries

1. Paired questions with identical contexts establish a behavioral composition gap (1,000 test sources).
2. Training-fitted native-state readouts recover bridge addresses within fixed native failures; attention-only and metadata controls identify the feature contribution. This is recoverability evidence.
3. Ranked-head knockout and wrong-paragraph calibration measure causal contributions to the output. Strong constituent effects locate shared evidence-use components.
4. The four-arm mass/pattern intervention identifies an accuracy interaction on 100 receivers from 25 discovery quadruplets. Those data provide a controlled diagnostic population; the separate 1,000-source address-calibration experiment provides broader evidence for access specificity.
5. Component comparisons and the 103 ambiguous-input control connect evidence selection, adaptation, and readout-based choices to repair performance.
6. Reciprocal exchanges preserve full-context word/character multisets before selection. Rerunning the complete pipeline tests its local fact dependence. This is a separate validation of repaired behavior.

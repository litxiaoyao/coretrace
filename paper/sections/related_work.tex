\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Multi-hop composition.} \citet{press2023compositionality} identify a gap between constituent recall and composition. Subsequent work examines delayed bridge resolution \citep{biran2024hopping}, competing relation logits \citep{yu2025back}, and probabilistic recall and selective extraction \citep{liu2026layerorder}. We study composition with both facts independently accessible.

\textbf{Contextual evidence use.} \citet{guo2025twohop} trace sequential querying in a synthetic Transformer. Retrieval heads \citep{wu2025retrieval} and document recognition \citep{zhang2026failure} relate attention to evidence access; multilingual work identifies bridge-routing heads \citep{kim2026bridge}. We distinguish address, access and reading.

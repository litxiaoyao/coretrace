\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Multi-hop composition.} \citet{press2023compositionality} identify the constituent--composition gap. Subsequent work examines delayed bridge resolution \citep{biran2024hopping}, competing relation logits \citep{yu2025back}, and probabilistic recall and selective extraction \citep{liu2026layerorder}. We study coordination of supplied evidence.

\textbf{Contextual evidence use.} \citet{guo2025twohop} trace sequential querying in a synthetic Transformer. Retrieval heads \citep{wu2025retrieval} and document recognition \citep{zhang2026failure} connect attention to evidence access. We separate address recovery, access and local reading.

\textbf{Mechanism-guided improvement.} Targeted interventions improve multilingual reasoning \citep{sun2026bridging,kim2026bridge}, in-context learning \citep{sun2025mappings}, persona expression \citep{qin2026persona}, and emotional information flow \citep{zhang2026emotional}. \method\ couples head adaptation with address-based evidence selection.

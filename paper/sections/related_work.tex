\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Multi-hop composition.} \citet{press2023compositionality} measure the gap between constituent and composed reasoning. \citet{biran2024hopping} identify delayed bridge resolution; \citet{yu2025back} address competing relation logits; \citet{liu2026layerorder} examine probabilistic recall and selective extraction. We study how accessible facts become usable when the evidence is supplied explicitly.

\textbf{Contextual evidence use.} \citet{guo2025twohop} trace sequential querying in a synthetic Transformer. Retrieval heads \citep{wu2025retrieval} and document recognition \citep{zhang2026failure} motivate analysis of evidence access. We distinguish recoverable addresses from paragraph access, then independently vary mass and conditional token weights to identify their interaction.

\textbf{Mechanism-guided improvement.} Targeted interventions have improved multilingual reasoning \citep{sun2026bridging,kim2026bridge}, in-context learning \citep{sun2025mappings}, persona expression \citep{qin2026persona}, and emotional information flow \citep{zhang2026emotional}. \method\ extends this approach to contextual composition by coupling evidence-access head adaptation with selection based on an internal address signal. Reciprocal fact exchanges link repair to changed local values.

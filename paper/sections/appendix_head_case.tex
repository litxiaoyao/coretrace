\section{Additional Evidence-Access Head Case}\label{app:head_case}
The Llama-3.1-8B head L13H18 provides a second measured example of the query-sensitive shift in \cref{fig:structure_function}. Under the composed question about the performer of \emph{Blame Canada}, it allocates 43.1\% of its attention mass to the root and 1.3\% to the bridge; when the direct question names Mary Kay Bergman, the masses become 4.3\% and 64.4\%, respectively. Calibrating all 40 selected heads changes the answer from Colorado to Los Angeles.

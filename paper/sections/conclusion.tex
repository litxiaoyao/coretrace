\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Correct constituent answers do not guarantee successful contextual composition. We find that bridge addresses often remain recoverable during failures, and that paragraph access changes the usefulness of relation-conditioned reading patterns. Reciprocal content interventions connect this account to the use of local evidence. \method\ turns these signals into an inference-time annotation-free repair with substantial composition gains and a specific benefit on ambiguous evidence. The findings concern the tested models and fixed two-hop panel; discovery overlap, development-selected scopes, and remaining constituent/capability losses define the limits of the present evidence.

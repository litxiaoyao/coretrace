\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\emph{Locate--Route--Read} connects contextual composition to measurable evidence use. Paired head profiles reveal root-to-bridge preference shifts; knockout establishes their causal role. Matched readouts and routing interventions connect available addresses to corrected answers, while crossed interventions expose joint-only corrections through coordinated local reading. \method\ applies this account to improve composed accuracy by 33.3--39.9 percentage points with fewer than 1\% trainable model parameters and to follow changed facts at stable evidence addresses. These results identify the coordination of location, access, and relation reading as a concrete target for improving composition.

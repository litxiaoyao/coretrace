\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We explain contextual composition through \emph{Locate--Route--Read}: representing the bridge address, routing attention to its paragraph, and reading the requested relation value. Causal localization and address readouts show that relevant information can remain available during composition failures. Crossed interventions reveal the central interaction: routing changes the usefulness of local reading. \method\ turns this account into targeted repair, improving composed accuracy by 33.3--39.9 percentage points with fewer than 1\% trainable model parameters. Case studies and reciprocal fact exchanges connect these gains to concrete evidence use, establishing the coordination of routing and reading as a practical target for improving composition.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Correct constituent answers and a recoverable bridge address can coexist with failed contextual composition. Our diagnosis identifies a functional interaction: paragraph access changes the behavioral utility of the local attention pattern. Address-specific controls establish the importance of allocating access to the relevant paragraph, while the crossed intervention quantifies how access and reading work together. \method\ uses this insight to combine evidence selection and selected-head adaptation, improving composed accuracy by 33.3--39.9 points with fewer than 1\% trainable parameters. Ambiguous-candidate comparisons connect the recovered address to useful decisions, and reciprocal exchanges show that the repaired system follows local fact changes. These findings make evidence access and local reading concrete, complementary targets for understanding and improving contextual two-hop reasoning.

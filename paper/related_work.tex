\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent multi-hop composition.}
The compositionality gap measures failed composition after successful constituent reasoning \citep{press2023compositionality}. \citet{biran2024hopping} identify delayed bridge resolution; their rescue rate selects a successful back-patching layer pair retrospectively for each example, leaving inference-time pair selection unresolved. \citet{yu2025back} diagnose competing relation-conditioned logits and add trainable cross-layer attention to feed deeper representations into earlier computation. \citet{liu2026layerorder} replace strict hop ordering with probabilistic recall and selective extraction; a mechanism-derived repair is absent from their evaluation. These studies analyze retrieval from model parameters. Our analysis separates access to supplied evidence from reading the relation-relevant content inside it.

\paragraph{Contextual evidence use.}
\citet{guo2025twohop} reconstruct a sequential-query circuit in a three-layer synthetic Transformer. Their pretrained-LLM experiments measure behavioral adaptation, leaving the proposed circuit without a causal test in those models. \citet{zhang2026failure} identify a position-dependent recognition bottleneck through instructions naming gold documents. The oracle locations jointly change evidence recognition and the search space, confounding their individual effects; the accompanying attention visualizations remain observational. Our shared-context interventions independently manipulate paragraph mass and its normalized within-paragraph distribution while retaining receiver value/output computations. Reciprocal object exchanges then test local evidence dependence with complete-context word and character counts held fixed.

\paragraph{Circuit-guided repair.}
\citet{sun2026bridging} trace multilingual failures to misaligned attention pathways over shared semantic neurons and fine-tune critical heads. \citet{kim2026bridge} identify multilingual bridge-routing heads and recover parametric failures by amplifying their outputs. \citet{sun2025mappings} localize heads that match new queries to demonstration labels and improve in-context classification through targeted head tuning. These methods intervene at the head or pathway level, leaving the interaction between evidence-paragraph access and conditional reading unmeasured. We resolve this interaction in contextual composition: bridge addresses remain recoverable during answer failure, and access calibration changes the effect of the local reading pattern. \method\ turns the address signal into evidence selection and combines it with scoped head adaptation.

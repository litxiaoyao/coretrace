\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent multi-hop composition.}
The compositionality gap measures failed composition after successful constituent reasoning \citep{press2023compositionality}. \citet{biran2024hopping} diagnose delayed bridge resolution, but their rescue rate retrospectively selects a successful back-patching layer pair per example. \citet{yu2025back} address competing relation logits through trainable cross-layer attention. \citet{liu2026layerorder} propose probabilistic recall and selective extraction without evaluating a mechanism-derived repair. These accounts concern parameterized knowledge; our intervention separates paragraph access from relation-conditioned reading of supplied evidence.

\paragraph{Contextual evidence use.}
\citet{guo2025twohop} reconstruct a sequential-query circuit in a three-layer synthetic Transformer; their pretrained-LLM experiments leave that circuit without causal verification. \citet{zhang2026failure} diagnose position-dependent recognition through instructions naming gold documents, jointly changing recognition and the search space. Their attention analysis remains observational. Our crossed interventions separately change paragraph mass and normalized local attention under fixed receiver value/output computations. Reciprocal object exchanges test local relation binding under identical complete-context word and character counts.

\paragraph{Circuit-guided repair.}
\citet{sun2026bridging} align multilingual pathways over shared semantic neurons by tuning critical heads. \citet{kim2026bridge} amplify bridge-routing heads to recover multilingual parametric failures. \citet{sun2025mappings} tune heads that match queries to demonstration labels in classification. \citet{qin2026persona} inject mean in-character head activations through Personality Signal Enhancement, while VEENA strengthens visual attention and selected emotional-neuron activations \citep{zhang2026emotional}. These interventions leave the interaction between paragraph mass and conditional reading unmeasured; we quantify it through crossed interventions and use recoverable bridge addresses to select evidence for an adapted model.

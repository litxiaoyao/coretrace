\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Composing factual knowledge.}
The compositionality gap measures failures to combine correctly answered subproblems \citep{press2023compositionality}. Mechanistic accounts of latent multi-hop reasoning trace bridge-entity resolution and answer extraction: back-patching exposes the importance of layer timing \citep{biran2024hopping}, while back attention addresses competing relation-specific signals \citep{yu2025back}. Recent work identifies layer-order inversion and models reasoning through probabilistic recall followed by selective extraction \citep{liu2026layerorder}. We study the corresponding gap when constituent and composed queries share explicit evidence. This setting makes the bridge paragraph and its local relation value available for controlled intervention, connecting the behavioral gap to identifiable evidence-use operations.

\paragraph{Accessing and using supplied evidence.}
Long-context studies establish position-sensitive evidence use \citep{liu2024lost}, and retrieval-head ablations identify attention components that support contextual retrieval \citep{wu2025retrieval}. In synthetic two-hop tasks, \citet{guo2025twohop} describe the emergence of a sequential-query mechanism. On natural multi-document questions, \citet{zhang2026failure} connect evidence visibility to composition through position-controlled attention instructions. Our paired queries reveal that an attention-derived bridge address can remain decodable during a failed answer. We examine how paragraph access and local reading interact by independently manipulating paragraph mass and the conditional attention pattern within it using receiver-computed values and original output-projection weights. The resulting interaction quantifies when a direct-query reading pattern becomes useful for composition.

\paragraph{From internal signals to targeted repair.}
Attention probes predict factual errors and constraint satisfaction \citep{yuksekgonul2024attention}. Mechanism-guided interventions improve multilingual multi-hop reasoning through selected-head tuning \citep{sun2026bridging} and amplification of bridge-routing heads \citep{kim2026bridge}. \method\ connects an internal paragraph-address readout to input-derived evidence selection and selected-head adaptation. Its ambiguity control measures where the readout changes evidence selection and answer accuracy; reciprocal value exchanges test whether repaired predictions follow the local facts. These experiments connect the diagnostic signal, its controlled manipulation, and its practical use within one contextual reasoning protocol.

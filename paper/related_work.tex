\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent multi-hop composition.}
The compositionality gap captures failed composition after successful constituent reasoning \citep{press2023compositionality}. \citet{biran2024hopping} diagnose delayed bridge resolution; \citet{yu2025back} address competing relation logits through trainable cross-layer attention; and \citet{liu2026layerorder} describe probabilistic recall and selective extraction. These accounts of parameterized knowledge motivate examining how available information becomes usable across a reasoning chain. We study this question with explicitly supplied facts, separating access to the bridge paragraph from the conditional reading of its contents.

\paragraph{Contextual evidence use.}
\citet{guo2025twohop} reconstruct a sequential-query circuit in a three-layer synthetic Transformer, connecting contextual two-hop reasoning to intermediate computations. \citet{zhang2026failure} examine position-dependent recognition of relevant documents. Our paired-query protocol compares composed and constituent questions under identical evidence, locating heads whose bridge access changes with query formulation. Supporting facts, distractors, and paragraph positions remain unchanged across queries. Crossed interventions then independently manipulate paragraph attention mass and its normalized token distribution, using receiver-computed values and original output-projection weights. This design reveals how access changes the usefulness of local reading.

\paragraph{Circuit-guided repair.}
\citet{sun2026bridging} align multilingual pathways over shared semantic neurons by tuning critical heads, while \citet{kim2026bridge} amplify bridge-routing heads in multilingual reasoning. \citet{sun2025mappings} tune heads that apply input--label mappings during in-context learning. Related interventions strengthen persona signals \citep{qin2026persona} and visual emotional information flow \citep{zhang2026emotional}. \method\ extends this explanation-to-intervention approach to contextual composition: the access--reading interaction identifies complementary targets for selected-head adaptation and evidence selection. Reciprocal fact exchanges further connect the resulting improvement to predictions that follow local relation values.
